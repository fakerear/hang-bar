Tap a set square when you finish it — the timer starts automatically. A beep and vibration signal the end of rest.

How to run this

Warm-up (5 min): arm circles, shoulder rolls, 10 slow bodyweight squats, 20s easy dead hang.

Progression: if you can't do a full pull-up, do negatives — jump to the top, lower yourself for 3–5 slow seconds. Once you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add one rep or one set.

Screen stays awake while the rest timer is running, so your phone won't lock mid-set.
